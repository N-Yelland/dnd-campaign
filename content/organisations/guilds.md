title: Guilds of Erran
order: 30

Guilds are a long-established way for the merchants and craftsmen of Erran to protect their trades, and access a wider network for finding new jobs and apprentices, all of which is particularly important in the light of the instability created by the Silverblight.

Some of these organisation can trace their foundation back to the Years of Darkness. Most will have a central "Guildhall" - a single location where the guild leadership can operate and host assemblies of the members.

## The Antiquarian's Guild
- Guildhall: Antiquarians' Hall, [great-burnton]


## The Angler's Guild
- Guildhall: Rivermen's Hall, [Lembrow]
