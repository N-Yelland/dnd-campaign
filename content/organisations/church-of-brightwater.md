# The Church of Brightwater


> "Let the darkness know: we are [the Light](lore/the-light) that does not yield."
>
> — Mother Superior [Agnessa Kandalah Yovahl](people/agnessa-kandalah-yovahl)

In the eyes of many, the Church of Brightwater was and continues to be the single most important organisation in the fight against the [Silverblight](lore/silverblight). In their Sanatoria they administer life-saving treatment to those afflicted, and in their Refineries they produce the priceless [Reversing Elixir](lore/reversing-elixir), the one substance capable of undoing the Silverblight's infection. In settlements across [Erran](locations) they work closely with [Blessed Defenders](lore/blessed-defenders), supporting each other to deliver their services more effectively to those most in need.

The endurance of the Church's relationship with the Blessed Defenders is seen through their worship of [the Blessed Saints](lore/blessed-saints). These are Blessed Defenders of years gone by who gave their lives in heroic acts of defence against the Blight, immortalised in prayers and hymns heard in the Church's ceremonies across Erran.

The Church does not follow a higher power per se, but a lot their teachings make reference to a nebulous concept known has "the Light". This is not a deity in the conventional sense; rather it is a personification of the hope that the people of Erran will overcome the Blight, and the knowledge that might make it possible.

## Locations

[Lightwarden Abbey](locations/plains/lightwarden-abbey) serves as the headquarters of the church, located in the Church's founding city of [Brightwater](locations/plains/brightwater). The Abbey overlooks the city from atop [St. Septa's Island](locations/plains/st-septa-s-island) in [Lake Vossar](locations/plains/lake-vossar). The Abbey includes its own Sanatorium and Refinery, but these are complemented by a network of similar establishments in settlements across [Erran](locations).

## Hierarchy

The acolytes of the Church who operate the Sanatoria and Refineries, generally referred to as "Sisters", may have different ranks among them based on seniority and competence. They can broadly be divided into three categories:

* **Sisters Attendant** are generally the lowest rank of acolytes of the Church. They receive training in basic medicine and work in Sanatoria, attempting to slow the progression of [Silverblight](lore/silverblight) in the patients, and eventually collecting [blightstones](lore/blightstones) from the deceased to be passed on to the Sisters Alchemical.
* The **Sisters Alchemical** are more experienced acolytes who have studied the arcane techniques for producing [Reversing Elixir](lore/reversing-elixir), and do so in specialised Refineries. They may spend many years studying, and often spend some time at the Library of [Lightwarden Abbey](locations/plains/lightwarden-abbey) to do so.
* Lastly, **Sisters Itinerant** work between Sanatoria and Refineries, responsible for securely and effectively transporting the blightstones and elixir to where they are needed most. Outside of this, they also perform evangelical duties and collect the much needed tithes that enable the Church to operate.

Irrespective of their role, they all answer to the establishment's **Mother Overseer**. Some of the Church's larger establishments may even have multiple Mothers Overseer in command.

Lightwarden Abbey hosts an additional Mother Overseer to whom the other Mothers Overseer report; she is named **Mother Superior**, and is the head of the Church. The incumbent is 89-year-old [Agnessa Kandalah Yovahl](people/agnessa-kandalah-yovahl). Responsibilities of the role include:

* Administration of Lightwarden Abbey and liaison with the Mayor of [Brightwater](locations/plains/brightwater).
* Hosting the annual [Church Congress](lore/church-congress), where the Mothers Overseer from across [Erran](locations) gather to take stock of the year past and discuss the plans for the year ahead.
* Managing the establishment of additional Refineries or Sanatoria.

When a Mother assumes her role, one of her first duties is the nomination of her successor from among the other sisters at her institution, or in the case of Mother Superior, from among the other Mothers Overseer of the Church. However, these nominations are kept secret, not revealed until the death or resignation of the post's occupant, and is free to be changed at any time.

Mothers that resign from their duties, generally due to age or illness, are known as **Mothers Ethereal**, and are granted permanent residence at the institution they served, should they want it.

### St. Agnessa, Keeper of the Well

Patron of Wells, Soldiers, and various towns in the [Grimpeaks](locations/valley-of-lem/the-grimpeaks). When a blighted horde descended on the area, they threatened to infect the water supply, risking the infection of a much larger area. Gathering other [Blessed Defenders](lore/blessed-defenders) from the surrounding villages, [Agnessa](people/st-agnessa) lead a last stand to defend the wellspring. Though the defence was ultimately successful, she was slain in battle against a fearsome [Blight-troll](creatures/blight-troll).

### St. Brinna, Shield of Courage

Patron of Shields, Accidents, and Refineries. During the demonstration of a new prototype of refining equipment, a fault caused a tank to rupture. This would have lead to the mixture of highly volatile solvents and an violent explosion of acid that would have melted the observers and anyone who happened to be walking in the street nearby... were it not for the quick actions of [Brinna](people/st-brinna), who used herself as a shield to seal the breach. This gave enough time for the area to be vacated and the tank emptied safely. But her exposure to the solvents had done significant damage, and she succumbed to her injuries shortly after.

### St. Cyril, the Wise

Patron of Scholarship, Writing, and [Port Rallian](locations/plains/port-rallian). Originally a student of [Old Imperial Languages](cultures/old-imperial-languages) at the [University of Port Rallian](organisations/university-of-port-rallian), the translation of the [Vilras Manuscript](lore/vilras-manuscript) was a call-to-arms for young [Cyril](people/st-cyril). He subsequently lead many expeditions deep into the [Blightwood](locations/blightwood) to uncover more information about [Reversing Elixir](lore/reversing-elixir) from Old Imperial ruins, and used the knowledge he uncovered to make key contributions to the Church's development of the refining process. He presumably met his end on a final expedition to [Vilras](locations/blightwood/vilras) from which he never returned, seeking the other half of [Mariah](people/st-mariah)'s manuscript,

### St. Eligius, the Watchman

Patron of Watchmen, Night, and the [Valley of Lem](locations/valley-of-lem). [Eligius](people/st-eligius) was head of the guard at the [Old Watchtower](locations/valley-of-lem/the-old-watchtower) in the Valley of Lem. Responsible for warning the towns in the valley of bands of [blighted creatures](creatures/blighted-wildlife) advancing over the [Grimpeaks](locations/valley-of-lem/the-grimpeaks), Eligius was on watch on the fateful night that "a thousand eyes looked up from the trees". The tower --- and soon the whole valley --- was quickly overrun by blighted forest creatures of every sort, but not before Eligius could light the beacon one final time.

### St. Jory, the Clarion

Patron of Music, Songs, and [location]. When [Jory](people/st-jory) and his fellow townsfolk woke to find their [town/city] now also home to several [giant bears](creatures/blighted-bears) that had been blinded by [the blight](lore/silverblight), they locked themselves in their houses. But realising that the bears could only hunt them by sound, Jory walked the streets ringing bells and singing the hymns of the Church to draw the entire pack to his location and lead them out of the [town/city]. While he did succeed in getting the bears out of the town, he himself could not escape them forever, and thus met a grisly end.

### St. Mariah, the Lightbringer

Patron of Adventure, Magic, and Ancient Relics. [Mariah](people/st-mariah) was a [Blessed](lore/the-blessed) Relic-seeker from [Lembrow](locations/valley-of-lem/lembrow), and arguably the most prolific of all time. But of all the ancient, magical, and mysterious artefacts she recovered from the ruins of the [Erranite Empire](history/erranite-empire), one stands above the rest in its significance. The [Vilras Manuscript](lore/vilras-manuscript) (as it is now known) --- an unassuming scrap of crumbling parchment taken from a sealed chamber high in a spire of the Ancient Imperial capital --- on translation suggested the existence of means of permanently reversing the effects of [Silverblight](lore/silverblight), and even hinted at how to make it. She brought it to [Brightwater](locations/plains/brightwater), which would spark the decades of experiments and research that led to the creation of the first vial of [Reversing Elixir](lore/reversing-elixir).

However, the manuscript was incomplete. While the experiments in Brightwater continued, she and the Blessed Adventurer [Cyril](people/st-cyril) set out on a second expedition to [Vilras](locations/blightwood/vilras) to recover the rest of the manuscript. They were never seen again.

### St. Septa, Mother of the Church

Patron of Buildings, Islands, and [Brightwater](locations/plains/brightwater). During the latter part of the [Years of Darkness](history/years-of-darkness), [St. Septa](people/st-septa) came to the city of Brightwater and founded the Church, becoming the first Mother Superior. Her full title is thus [Blessed](lore/the-blessed) Mother Superior Saint Septa Iridessa Morhaim. She is one of few figures from the Years of Darkness whose existence is well-documented. While she did not meet an untimely end in battle against some blighted foe, or in sacrifice for the lives of others, she has been remembered never the less for her foundational teachings that make up the ideology of the Church today.
