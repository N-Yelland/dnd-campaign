# The Aldovian Academy

> “Through Strength and Knowledge, Salvation”
>
> – Motto of the Academy

The Aldovian Academy sits on an island of jagged rocks in [Klathras Bay](locations/plains/klathras-bay), jutting out of the waves that crash into the surrounding cliffs. Here, young [Blessed](lore/the-blessed) are trained in martial combat, medicine, herbalism, alchemy, and other essential skills for [Blessed Defenders](lore/blessed-defenders) to protect villages and towns from the ever-encroaching [Blight](lore/silverblight).

## History

The Academy was founded by [Olovar Aldov](people/olovar-aldov) in 39YL, as **Olovar Aldov's Academy for the Blessed Children of Erran**, back when the [Blightwood](locations/blightwood) stretched all the way along the [Spearhead Cliffs](locations/valley-of-lem/spearhead-cliffs) to the sides of the [Fairen gorge](locations/plains/r-fairen). Aldov, an ex-mercenary and retired [Blessed Defender](lore/blessed-defenders) of [Klathras](locations/plains/klathras), saw the pressing need to pass on his skills to the next generation.

Aldov was neither an academic nor an educator; records suggest that his methods as the first "Archdean" were brutal and draconian. Nevertheless, the Academy quickly gained a reputation for producing excellent fighters and healers. Word spread, and soon people from far and wide sought their children's education at the Academy, that they might return a few years later with the skills necessary to help defend their hometown.

## Admissions

The **Registrar** is responsible for student admissions. They work with emissaries from [klathrassian-bureaucracy] travelling village to village seeking potential students, who must be proven [Blessed](lore/the-blessed) of at least eleven years of age.

An academy education is highly sought after. Numerous incidents of parents trying to get their non-Blessed children admitted have led to the development of the [Registration Ritual](lore/registration-ritual), wherein the child is tested to prove their Blessed status. This demands honesty, as a negative test results in infection, which may lead to death if treatment is unavailable or unsuccessful. Even when the test comes back positive, it is not a painless procedure, and it leaves a scar known as the **"[Mark of the Blessed](lore/mark-of-the-blessed)"**, which surely identifies them as students of the Academy.

## Structure

In addition to the Archdean, Bursar, Registrar, and Groundskeeper, the Faculty includes four Deans that each run one of the following "schools" within the Academy:

- School of the Physical Arts
    - Close Combat
    - Ranged Combat
    - Strength and Conditioning
- School of Healing and Restorative Magic
    - Herbalism
    - Medicine
    - Restorative Magic
- School of Wilderness Survival
    - Natural History
    - Foraging
    - Geography
- School of Blight Sciences
    - Only a single subject, taught in the final year. 
    - This course culminates in a “practical examination” wherein students are deposited deep in a region of blighted woodland and left to fend for themselves.
