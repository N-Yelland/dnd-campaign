# The Klathrassian Bureaucracy

Originally the governing organisation of the city of [Klathras](locations/plains/klathras), the Bureaucracy has expanded its influence to surrounding towns and cities. Its headquarters are the imposing [Grand Sanctum](locations/plains/grand-sanctum) in Klathras. Officials, or bureaucrats, are frequently dispatched to execute the orders of the moment. The exact internal power structure is really only understood by the bureaucrats themselves, and even then, it is complicated.

Numerous offices exist to perform various functions:
- Department of Catalogues and Indices
    - The cataloguing of such relics
    - The governance of the distribution and usership of such relics
    - The cataloguing of other things – flora and fauna, history and geography
    - The survey of populations for [blight](lore/silverblight) and other issues
- Department of Observations and Expeditions
    - The survey for [blessed](lore/the-blessed) individuals, in partnership with [the Academy](organisations/aldovian-academy)
    - The survey of newly discovered arcane relics
    - The analysis and investigation of said relics
    - The liaison with distant peoples, on trade or other affairs
- Department of Internal Logistics
    - The calculation of taxation requirements
    - The implementation of taxation systems
    - The distribution of funds internally
    - The distribution and authentication of official documents
    - The provision of security for the Inner Sanctum and relics
    - The management of internal offices themselves and the assignment of bureaucrats.

Bureaucrats are dedicated to their work. They often use their Bureaucratic Designation (BD) in place of a name when signing documents and introducing themselves in an official capacity. This is an alphanumeric code that encodes their office, status, and role, starting with "B" for "Bureaucrat". There are similar codes for assets and artefacts (A), non-Bureaucrat  citizens \(C), and Bureaucratic Documents (D).
