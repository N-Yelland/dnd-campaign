Hidden away in the undergrowth, the [Gnomish](cultures/gnomes) community of Nim's Hollow manages to thrive amidst [the Blight](lore/silverblight). This is in no small part thanks to [the Blessed](lore/the-blessed) family of [Fallowgrove](people/fallowgrove-family).

The Fallowgroves are descendants of the legendary Gnomish druid, [Grandfather Nim](people/grandfather-nim). His magic appears to flow through the family still, seemingly guaranteeing at least one Blessed child each generation. They continue to teach their children and community the arts of druidcraft, and work with [Lembrow](locations/valley-of-lem/lembrow) in protecting the valley.
