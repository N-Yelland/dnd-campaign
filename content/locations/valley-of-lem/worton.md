For generations the [Gnomes](cultures/gnomes) of Worton have developed an expertise in local plants and herbs with medicinal and alchemical uses.

The pungent ointments and salves created by the skilled village elders are thought to be a significant reason that the village has survived the encroaching [Blightwood](locations/blightwood) without a [Blessed Defender](lore/blessed-defenders) to its name.
