During the [Years of Darkness](history/years-of-darkness), a particularly extreme sect of the [Order of Scarlet](organisations/order-of-scarlet) vehemently believed the [Silverblight](lore/silverblight) to be a punishment sent by the [Old Gods](lore/old-gods), and that it could only be abated by inflicting greater punishment on ourselves. Hence the red stains on the steps that lead up to the raised central courtyard that give the temple its name today.

Nowadays, the resident monks have a very different purpose: farming. Regular offerings to [Orim](lore/orim) and [Onu](lore/onu), the Gods of the Earth and Sky, have ensured bountiful harvests year after year, and their blessings continue to ward off the encroaching [Blightwood](locations/blightwood) as nearby lands are taken by the disease.

Many of the surrounding towns and villages have become dependent on the temple's reliable supply of healthy produce, and management of the temple's logistics and commitments has become more complicated. Arguments between the high priests are not uncommon &mdash; their expertise does not generally extend to matters of commerce.
