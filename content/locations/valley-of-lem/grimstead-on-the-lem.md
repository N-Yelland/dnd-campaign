What is now a crumbling ruin, overtaken by the crawling vines and twisted trees of the [Blightwood](locations/blightwood), was once the seat of the [Grand Duchy of Lem](history/grand-duchy-of-lem). This was during the [Years of Darkness](history/years-of-darkness), at a time before [the Blight](lore/silverblight) had spread to the Valley proper and was still held back by the [Grimpeaks](locations/valley-of-lem/the-grimpeaks).

It was during the period of the Duchy of Lem that the [Old Watchtower](locations/valley-of-lem/the-old-watchtower) was constructed. Designed to provide vision of the nearby Blightwood, it was used to gain early warning and observation of advancing bands of [blighted creatures](creatures/blighted-wildlife).

Even when it was known the fight was lost, the people struggled until the end until Blight consumed them all, including the last Duke and Duchess themselves.
