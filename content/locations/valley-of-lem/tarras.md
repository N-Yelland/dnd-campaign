In the shadow of the imposing [Mount Ereg](locations/valley-of-lem/mt-ereg) can be found the graceful towers and pavilions of Tarras. In the time of the [Old Empire](history/erranite-empire), it was made rich by its proximity to the [Bluestone](lore/bluestone) quarries in the [Grimpeaks](locations/valley-of-lem/the-grimpeaks) and gemstone mines in the [Erranite mountains](locations/valley-of-lem/the-erranite-mts).

Following the [Empire's fall](history/fall-of-the-empire), the city's remoteness made it an excellent home for a community of [Tieflings](cultures/tieflings) ostracised from their previous homelands. This was not an uncommon occurrence: the Tiefling species possesses a considerably stronger resistance to [the Blight](lore/silverblight), which was often connected in a negative light to their otherworldly appearance and the belief that the Blight is a punishment sent by the Gods.
