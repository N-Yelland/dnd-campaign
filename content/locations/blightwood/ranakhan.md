If one were to sail up the [river Ranathar](locations/blightwood/r-ranathar)'s large estuary today, you would still be overlooked by the same great towers and statues that lined the waterway when it was filled with Imperial ships bearing cargo and visitors from across [Erran](locations) and beyond.

Ranakhan was the [Empire](history/erranite-empire)'s centre for trade and diplomacy. [Dragonborn](cultures/dragonborn) from [Ajrazan](locations/beyond-erran/ajrazan), [Elves](cultures/elves) from [Astmon](locations/plains/astmon), [Tieflings](cultures/tieflings) from the [Isle of Torment](locations/beyond-erran/isle-of-torment), and [Aasimar](cultures/aasimar) from the mysterious continent of [Olum](locations/beyond-erran/olum) --- all came to Ranakhan to marvel at the Empire's opulence and grandeur, and barter at the markets to take home some part of it.
