Beyond a few crumbling foundations and heaps of blackened stone, little remains of what was once a beautiful and vibrant place. Everel, the original home of the [Halflings](cultures/halflings), was one of the oldest inhabited locations in [Erran](locations). It certainly predates any of the [Old Empire](history/erranite-empire)'s cities; some say the Halflings lived in this valley since the day the Gods created them.

However, as the [Silverblight](lore/silverblight) was consuming the great city of [Ranakhan](locations/blightwood/ranakhan) and the surrounding towns to the north, it quickly spread to the Halfling settlements in Everel. Fearing the worst, the Elders make the reckless decision to torch the forest and their homes to cleanse the blight and buy them time to escape its clutches.

Those Halflings that survived fled through the [Pass of Shadows](locations/blightwood/pass-of-shadows) in the [Valley of Lem](locations/valley-of-lem) and beyond. Not all the clans survived their exodus. Some journeyed for miles, eventually settling in distant towns and cities. Others maintained their nomadic existence, either coming to peace with their new way of life, or continuing the search for answers, and for justice.
