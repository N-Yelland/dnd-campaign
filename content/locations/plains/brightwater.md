On a peaceful night, the shining lights in the marble towers of the City of Brightwater are reflected perfectly in the serene waters of [Lake Vossar](locations/plains/lake-vossar), echoing the stars of the heavens above. Home of the eponymous [Church of Brightwater](organisations/church-of-brightwater), the skyline is dominated by the towering white and blue facades of [Lightwarden Abbey](locations/plains/lightwarden-abbey) standing proud on [St. Septa's Island](locations/plains/st-septa-s-island), accessible only via the [Bridge of The Saints](locations/plains/bridge-of-the-saints).

Lightwarden abbey includes many of city's important locations. Its huge [sanatorium](organisations/church-of-brightwater#section-sanatoria) is the largest there is; the sick may journey many miles to be treated here, despite the volume of patients it takes in, it offers one of the best [Silverblight](lore/silverblight) survival rates too. This is likely because of the vast [refinery](organisations/church-of-brightwater#section-refineries) complex right next door. In addition to producing a steady flow of [reversing elixir](lore/reversing-elixir), the [Mothers Overseer](organisations/church-of-brightwater#section-hierarchy) at the Abbey's refinery spend their time educating the next generation of Sisters Alchemical.

The Abbey's library is an eminent educational establishment in its own right; it is home to the world's largest collection of texts and records from the [Old Empire](history/erranite-empire). The [Great Chapel](locations/plains/great-chapel) holds daily rites and ceremonies, often conducted by the Church's Mother Superior. This includes the annual [Church Congress](lore/church-congress), where the Mothers Overseer from across [Erran](locations) come to discuss the Church's future goals and challenges.

Much of the rest of the city has grown up in support of the Abbey. The shops and stalls of the city offers the finest money can buy when it comes to candles, incense, and other religious paraphernalia.
