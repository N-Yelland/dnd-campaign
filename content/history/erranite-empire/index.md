> OUR BELOVED EMPEROR, WHOSE WISDOM, MERCY, AND DEVOTION TO THE PEOPLE OF ERRAN WERE WTHOUT EQUAL, SHALL NOT BE FORGOTTEN.
>
> -- From the inscription on the tomb of Emperor Volathros II (transl. Nestor Yorfe)

The **Erranite Empire** (often called the **Old** or **Ancient Empire**) was a powerful realm led by a elite society of humans who wielded incredibly advanced magic. For nearly a thousand years it dominated the continent of Erran (from which it takes its name) until its fall shortly following the outbreak of the [silverblight] plague.

Almost all concrete information concerning the Old Empire has been obtained from texts recovered from Imperial Ruins, generally at great risk to the recoverers. The Ancient Imperial Language is far from straightforward: there are few scholars capable of accurate translation. For this reason, much of Imperial history is still most speculation.
