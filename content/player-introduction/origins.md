# Lore & Your Origins


> In 5.5e, your character's starting abilities and proficiencies are determined by a combination of class, species, and background. This section is intended to show how different species and backgrounds might manifest within this setting.
>
> None of this is meant to be particularly proscriptive, and there are plenty of gaps where you should feel free to make more stuff up! In particular, treat no list as exhaustive!

You were born sometime in the first few centuries of the [Years of Longing](history/years-of-longing) (YL), an age of fragile peace in [Erran](locations) following the wars and desolation of the [Years of Darkness](history/years-of-darkness) that came before. [Silverblight](lore/silverblight) still ravages the land, but there is just enough fight and determination in the people to bring hope of a future for civilisation.

## Religion & Governance


Your "recruiter", [Mariah Fairford](people/mariah-fairford), is the [Mother Overseer](organisations/church-of-brightwater#section-hierarchy) of the [Lembrow Sanatorium](locations/valley-of-lem/lembrow-sanatorium), an establishment of the **[Church of Brightwater](organisations/church-of-brightwater)**. This is a major religious order originating in [Brightwater](locations/plains/brightwater) during the [Years of Darkness](history/years-of-darkness), and now spread throughout [Erran](locations). They worship the **[Blessed Saints](lore/blessed-saints)**, once mortal [Blessed](lore/the-blessed) individuals who, generations ago, sacrificed themselves heroically in the enduring fight against [the Blight](lore/silverblight). Their ascent to divine status is made possible by the focussed worship of the Church.

*Perhaps you were a devotee of the church. Where did you work? What made you leave your position?*

- **Attendants** work in [sanatoria](organisations/church-of-brightwater#section-sanatoria) to care for afflicted patients. While rarely can they cure the disease, they can at least ease one's suffering… [Acolyte]
- **Alchemicals** work in [refineries](organisations/church-of-brightwater#section-refineries), and learn the advanced techniques to produce [reversing elixir](lore/reversing-elixir), the only known cure to Silverblight. [Artisan, Sage, Scribe]
- **Itinerants** work between the two, and collect the all-important alms and charity to allow the Church's vital work to continue. [Soldier, Guide]

The other major religious organisation is the monastic **[Order of Scarlet](organisations/order-of-scarlet)**. They believe the Blight to be a punishment from the Gods, and as such attempt to appease them in prayer, repentance, and even sacrifice. Compared to the Church of Brightwater, they are more dispersed and less structured, with each temple operating in isolation, both from each other and wider society.

*Perhaps you were once an acolyte of one of these temples. What drew you out of seclusion? Exactly what you did will depend on the God you worshipped...*

- **[Ithikku](lore/ithikku)** and **[Akkamon](lore/akkamon)** are the twin Gods of Chaos and Order. Their blessings grant power and might, that might turn priests into battle-monks, ready to fight off whatever the Blight sends. [Soldier, Hermit]
- **[Onu](lore/onu)** is the God of the Sky, **[Orim](lore/orim)** is God of the Earth. Wards and blessings of Onu ensure good rains for crops and a bountiful harvest. Surplus crops from such consecrated farmland are a lifeline for nearby towns and villages with farms decimated by the plague. [Farmer, Hermit]
- **[Ghelothar](lore/ghelothar)** is the God of the Mountains... **[Varothar](lore/varothar)** is God of the Sea.

Beyond religious groups, the closest thing to "government" in the [Years of Longing](history/years-of-longing) is the **[Klathrassian Bureaucracy](organisations/klathrassian-bureaucracy)**. Headquartered in the imposing [Grand Sanctum](locations/plains/grand-sanctum) of the city of **[Klathras](locations/plains/klathras)**, their original purpose was to ensure that the city remains Blight-free through meticulous surveys of populations and diligent record keeping. In recent years their scope has expanded to analysis of Silverblight throughout most of Erran.

The Bureaucracy also administrate the search for relics of the [Old Empire](history/erranite-empire), and carefully document the findings, in the hope that someone unearthed from the past that might help in the fight against Blight in the future.

*Perhaps you were once employed by the Bureaucracy, either directly or indirectly. In which department did you work? Did you abandon your station? Why?*

- The Department of Catalogues and Indices keep records of everything they can (there's even a catalogue for catalogues!). [Scribe, Sage]
- The Department of Observations and Expeditions works with various parties to survey nearby land and maintain relationships with more distant peoples. [Guide, Guard, Merchant, Sailor]

While there are no hereditary titles, senior bureaucrats in the **Department of Internal Logistics** are well-remunerated for their coin-counting competence; they and their families are the closest that Klathras has to nobility [Noble]. The relative wealth that the bureaucracy brings the city mean it is a much-beloved hub for the creative arts, in a world where most have little time for such frivolities [Artisan, Entertainer]. It can be equally appealing for those looking to take advantage [Charlatan, Criminal].

Outside of large cities, day-to-day governance for villages and towns comes from local rulers: elected Chiefs and Mayors and unelected Lords and Ladies whose children inherit their titles [Noble]. The temperament and regimes of these leaders can vary greatly, but generally they should provide some form of protection from Silverblight if they expect to hold their power for any length of time [Guard, Soldier].

## Education & Magic


Graduates of the **[Aldovian Academy](organisations/aldovian-academy)**, [Klathras](locations/plains/klathras), are highly sought out to offer such protection. Dedicated to training the next generation of [Blessed Defenders](lore/blessed-defenders) in the skills necessary to fend of [Blighted creatures](creatures/blighted-wildlife), and handle the effects of their attacks, the Academy accepts only children proven to be [Blessed](lore/the-blessed).

*Perhaps you were a student or teacher at the Academy. What did you teach, or what was your favourite subject? The Academy is divided into four schools...*

- **School of the Physical Arts**: close/ranged combat, strength and conditioning. [Soldier, Guard]
- **School of Healing and Restorative Magic**: herbalism, medicine, and restorative magic. [Sage, Acolyte?]
- **School of Wilderness Survival**: natural history, foraging, geography and cartography. [Sage, Guide, Scribe]

There's also the **School of Blight Sciences**, but it only teaches a single subject, only to students in their final year, which culminates an "exam" in which students are deposited in the [Blightwood](locations/blightwood) and assessed on their ability to fend for themselves.

Other educational establishments include the **[University of Port Rallian](organisations/university-of-port-rallian)** and the library of **[Lightwarden Abbey](locations/plains/lightwarden-abbey)**, [Brightwater](locations/plains/brightwater). Far from their intellectual height under the [Old Empire](history/erranite-empire), they now focus their limited resources on researching [Silverblight](lore/silverblight) and the history of the Empire it defeated. [Sage, Scribe, Guide]

While most knowledge of Magic was lost with the Old Empire, one institution of magical education remains: the secretive **[Drayford College](organisations/drayford-college)**. Outside its walls, any magical knowledge must have been self-taught, possibly obtained from studying old arcane inscriptions, or come from some innate or otherworldly ability.

Despite the best efforts of the [Church](organisations/church-of-brightwater) and the Bureaucracy, for many, death by blight remains inevitable, and outbreaks are far too likely. It is not uncommon for whole families or villages to find themselves reduced to nothing in a matter of weeks, perhaps only the Blessed surviving [Hermit, Wayfarer].

However, for some unlucky souls Death never comes (or at least, not before she is due). While they bear the crystalline scars and terribly pallor, they lack many of the symptoms and do not progress to the final stage: delirium. Such people are called **[half-Blessed](lore/the-half-blessed)**, though really it is more of a curse: while they themselves may be unaffected, they still have the ability to infect others. This often leads to their ostracism from society. Could this be you?

## Elves & Dwarves


The **[Elves](cultures/elves)**, with their luxuriant lifespans and affinity with nature, consider themselves to be more susceptible to [Silverblight](lore/silverblight) than other species: they are much less open to the rest of [Erran](locations) now than during the reign of the [Old Empire](history/erranite-empire), who were a close ally. Fortunately, the sylvan groves they call home are across the continent from the spreading blight.

Despite this, the Elvish Lords [Noble] continue to send [Blessed](lore/the-blessed) Elves to the border regions of their lands as “Guardians” to “fend off” any Blighted onslaught [Soldier, Guard]. Though it is not unheard of for Elven Guardians to take pity on the suffering land they see before them, and leave their posts, to seek out more meaningful opportunities [Hermit].

**[Dwarves](cultures/dwarves)'** natural hardiness seems to make them more resilient to the Blight, and means there is a greater proportion of Blessed among them. In many ways their lives are unchanged from during the [Years of Darkness](history/years-of-darkness) and before, able to produce works of art, culture, and craftsmanship [Artisan, Merchant, Entertainer].

However, Dwarves are similarly reluctant to associate with humans. Many attribute this to mistreatment at the hands of the Old Empire, which was only a few generations ago in the lives of Dwarves. They are slow to forgive past transgressions, and while it would be unfair (to most) to say that they are grateful for the arrival of Silverblight, they do not wish to see a return to the old ways. Despite this, there are some who are willing to look past historic wrongs and take up arms together, against the Blight.

## Halflings & Gnomes


The homeland of the **[Halflings](cultures/halflings)** was overcome by [the blight](lore/silverblight) not long after the [Old Empire](history/erranite-empire) had fallen. Few in number, those that remain exist as refugees or nomads, seeking to a return to a stable way of life. [Hermit, Wayfarer, Criminal]

**[Gnomes](cultures/gnomes)** are pervasive and persistent, existing often unseen, with their small, tightly knit communities able to slip through the cracks. That's not to say that they haven't been affected by the blight, but their ingenuity has allowed them to remain in lands long abandoned by other folk. [Guide, Hermit]

## Exotic Species


**[Dragonborn](cultures/dragonborn)** and **[Tieflings](cultures/tieflings)** are not native to these lands. But at the zenith of the [Old Empire](history/erranite-empire)'s expanse, trade and diplomacy with all these races was commonplace, and families of these folk settled in the port towns and cities [Merchant, Sailor, Artisan]. When [the blight](lore/silverblight) came, many will have returned to their homeland while they still could; later ships would be sent back, or worse scuttled fearing the spread of the disease. Thus, a few will remain, perhaps dreaming of their homeland. Occasionally, newcomers arrive, though why they choose this blighted land is known only to themselves!
- The home of the Dragonborn is the rocky, sun-soaked archipelago of **[Ajrazan](locations/beyond-erran/ajrazan)**.
- The home of the Tieflings is the storm-ridden **[Isle of Torment](locations/beyond-erran/isle-of-torment)**.

**[Goliaths](cultures/goliaths)** come from the [Northern Wastes](locations/beyond-erran/northern-wastes), and **[Aasimar](cultures/aasimar)** from a mysterious continent in the south (where?). Both are exceptionally rare in [Erran](locations).

*That's not to say you couldn't be one… but then, why are you here?*
