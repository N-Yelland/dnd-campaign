The Great Blightwood
====================

.. location:: Vilras
    :desc: Ancient Capital of the Erranite Empire
    :coords: 0.26042 0.15315
    :type: ruin
    :label_offset: left

    Gravity-defying Bluestone spires sprout from the dense forest like hands reaching up to the heavens, each of the many "fingers" intricately carved with the runic language of their creators. But there is no one left to read them.

    Vilras was the largest and oldest city of the Ancient Erranite Empire. At the empire's peak, just before the fall, it was home to the worlds largest repositories of arcane knowledge and power. It is this power that has protected the stones of the city itself from crumbling to rubble in the subsequent centuries.

    Thought to be the originating point of Silverblight, Vilras is unsurprisingly located in the least hospitable region of the Blightwood. Seldom do mortals venture so far; what little we know of the city is drawn from stories and legends of Blessed Adventurers long departed.

.. location:: Ranakhan
    :desc: Imperial Gateway to the World Beyond Erran
    :coords: 0.25378 0.23418
    :type: ruin
    :label_offset: above
    
    If one were to sail up the river Ranathar's large estuary today, you would still be overlooked by the same great towers and statues that lined the waterway when it was filled with Imperial ships bearing cargo and visitors from across Erran and beyond.

    Ranakhan was the Empire's centre for trade and diplomacy. Dragonborn from Ajrazan, Elves from Astmon, Tieflings from the Isle of Torment, and Aasimar from the mysterious continent of Olum --- all came to Ranakhan to marvel at the Empire's opulence and grandeur, and barter at the markets to take home some part of it.

.. location:: Everel
    :desc: Charred Ruins of the Ancestral Halfling Home
    :coords: 0.31784 0.30861
    :type: ruin
    
    Beyond a few crumbling foundations and heaps of blackened stone, little remains of what was once a beautiful and vibrant place. Everel, the original home of the Halflings, was one of the oldest inhabited locations in Erran. It certainly predates any of the Old Empire's cities; some say the Halflings lived in this valley since the day the Gods created them.

    However, as the Silverblight was consuming the great city of Ranakhan and the surrounding towns to the north, it quickly spread to the Halfling settlements in Everel. Fearing the worst, the Elders make the reckless decision to torch the forest and their homes to cleanse the blight and buy them time to escape its clutches.

    Those Halflings that survived fled through the Pass of Shadows in the Valley of Lem and beyond. Not all the clans survived their exodus. Some journeyed for miles, eventually settling in distant towns and cities. Others maintained their nomadic existence, either coming to peace with their new way of life, or continuing the search for answers, and for justice.
