For generations the Gnomes of Worton have developed an expertise in local plants and herbs with medicinal and alchemical uses.

The pungent ointments and salves created by the skilled village elders are thought to be a significant reason that the village has survived the encroaching Blightwood without a Blessed Defender to its name.
