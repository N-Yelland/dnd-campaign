What is now a crumbling ruin, overtaken by the crawling vines and twisted trees of the Blightwood, was once the seat of the Grand Duchy of Lem. This was during the Years of Darkness, at a time before the Blight had spread to the Valley proper and was still held back by the Grimpeaks.

It was during the period of the Duchy of Lem that the Old Watchtower was constructed. Designed to provide vision of the nearby Blightwood, it was used to gain early warning and observation of advancing bands of blighted creatures.

Even when it was known the fight was lost, the people struggled until the end until Blight consumed them all, including the last Duke and Duchess themselves.

**Notable Residents:**

* Duke Garrus III, Protector of the Valley
