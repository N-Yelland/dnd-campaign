Where the gorge carved by the River Fairen breaks through into the Bay of Tears, several tall islands erupt from the waves with steep, jagged cliffs on all sides. Atop the largest of these islands sits the crumbling ruins of the Ancient temple complex of Klathras, some parts of which predate even Erranite rule.

Following its abandonment after the fall of the Empire, it has grown into the most populous settlement in Erran. Now the citizens inhabit all the old temple buildings. Some rise above the city in squat blocky towers, others are carved into the rock itself with windows built into the cliff face itself.

To ensure the populations survival, a strict quarantine policy is maintained. While this is made easier by the city's inaccessible location, it is also administered by the Klathrassian Bureaucracy, the closest these lands come to organised government. The Bureaucracy is based in the imposing Grand Sanctum, whose impenetrable inner halls are only accessible by the senior bureaucrats.

A smaller adjacent island is home to the Aldovian Academy, which takes in young Blessed children from across Erran as students, to be trained in all the essential skills for Blessed defenders: combat, medicine, alchemy, etc.

With Silverblight only a concern of distant lands, the citizens are free to revel in more frivolous pursuits. Klathras has become a hub for the arts, both creative and performative.

**Notable Locations:**

* The Grand Sanctum
* Rose Garden Temple (Order of Scarlet)
* Olovar Aldov's Academy for the Blessed Children of Erran


**Notable Residents:**

* Borras Ilwinter, Registrar of the Aldovian Academy
